TCU Turns 20 with Color Fun Run at TLC Park

More than 2,000 Taguig City University (TCU) students, faculty members, staff, and alumni took part in a three-kilometer Color Fun Run at TLC Park on Sunday, September 20, to celebrate the University's 20th Founding Anniversary.

The run started and ended at the TLC Park Concert Grounds along Laguna Lake Highway in Barangay Lower Bicutan. Before setting off, participants joined a Zumba warm-up led by the TCU Pep Squad.

Among those who joined the celebration were TCU President Raymundo Arcega, Vice President for Administration and Finance Atty. Aiko Mejia, Vice President for Academic Affairs Dr. Alberto D. Madrigal, University Secretary Atty. Marlon Raquel, Councilor Marisse Balina-Eron, and Councilor Kim Abbang.

For Atty. Raquel, the run offered a chance to look back on how far the University has come. He also thanked the city government for its continued support:“Today, we run not simply to reach the finish line. We run to celebrate how far TCU has come. For 20 years, our university has continued to move forward, overcoming challenges, embracing opportunities, and touching the lives of thousands of students and families."

In her message, Councilor Balina-Eron said the event also gave members of the TCU community a chance to exercise and get to know one another: “Ang fun run ay isa sa pinakamagandang ehersisyo para lumakas tayo, lumusog tayo, at siyempre, para magkaroon tayo ng camaraderie at mas makilala natin ang bawat isa. Sa ngalan ni Mayor Lani Cayetano, binabati ko kayo ng happy 20th founding anniversary, Taguig City University!"

That sense of community stood out to second-year tourism management student Jerlyn Jane Salvador, who said the warm-up, the guidance along the route, and the number of fellow runners kept her motivated: “Masaya po kasi maayos po ang programa. Enjoyable po ang warm-up na pinangunahan ng Pep Squad, accommodating po ang mga gumabay sa amin sa route, at marami po talagang sumali kaya motivated kang tumakbo."

For Michelle Mendoza, 18, a secondary education student joining her first Color Fun Run, the best moment came at the end of the route: “Masaya po at worth it ang pagod. Highlight po para sa akin ang pagdating ko sa finish line—the best feeling! Sana po magkaroon ulit ng ganitong event na masaya, makulay, at nakakatulong para maging active kami."

She thanked Mayor Cayetano for the scholarship support that allows her to pursue her chosen course without worrying about tuition.
