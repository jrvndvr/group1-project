ABOUT US

Taguig City University provides students with opportunities to learn, develop their skills, and prepare for their future careers. Through education and different learning experiences, students can grow academically and personally.

The university community also takes pride in student participation in cultural, scientific, technological, and sports activities at local, national, and international levels.

Our Smart Attendance System project explores how technology can make attendance recording easier and more organized. It aims to reduce the time spent checking attendance manually and help maintain clear attendance records.
